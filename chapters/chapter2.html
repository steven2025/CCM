    核心概念 · Concept

    Power distance is about accepted inequality

    权力距离关注对权力不平等的接受

    Power distance concerns the extent to which unequal power is expected and accepted.权力距离关注人们在多大程度上预期并接受权力的不平等分配。
    Lower power distance · Authority can be questioned; participation is more expected.较低权力距离：权威可以被质疑，更期待参与和讨论。
    Higher power distance · Status and hierarchy carry more weight in decisions.较高权力距离：地位和层级在决策中具有更大分量。
    Respecting someone is not the same as never questioning them.尊重某个人，不等于永远不能提出不同意见。

      Risk-taking depends on the domain

      敢不敢冒险，要看具体领域

      Hsee and Weber asked American and Chinese participants to predict each other’s risk preferences. Predictions and measured choices did not simply coincide.奚恺元与韦伯请美国和中国参与者预测彼此的风险偏好。直觉判断与实际测量并不简单一致。
      Investment decisions: Chinese participants were more risk-seeking in the reported study.投资决策：该研究中，中国参与者表现出更高的风险寻求。
      Medical and academic decisions: the same between-group difference was not found.医疗与学业决策：研究未发现同样的组间差异。
      Before saying “this culture is more risk-taking,” ask: in what domain, with what consequences and support?说“这种文化更敢冒险”之前，先问：在哪个领域？有什么后果和支持条件？

      Domain-specific findings领域特定的研究发现

      Hsee & Weber (1999), Study 2: investment, medical and academic decisions. The original PPT’s financial-versus-social summary should not be treated as a universal ranking of national risk-taking.Hsee与Weber（1999）研究2比较投资、医疗与学业决策。原PPT关于经济与社会领域的概括，不应作为国民冒险程度的普遍排名。

      Make fairness explainable让公平的依据能够被解释

      Applying the rule protects consistency but can overlook relevant circumstances. A private exception offers care but raises questions of equal treatment.
